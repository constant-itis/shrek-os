Shrek OS wallpapers
===================

Original artwork made for Shrek OS — Japanese-woodblock (ukiyo-e / shin-hanga)
takes on the swamp, styled to match popular terminal/editor color palettes so a
wallpaper pairs naturally with the matugen-derived dynamic theme. First-party
assets, part of the Shrek OS project and covered by the repository license; NOT
third-party art (unlike Omarchy's set, these ship with known provenance).

Shipped as JPG (1672x941) — the sealed image renders JPG with no extra Qt image
plugin. The DMS wallpaper switcher is pointed here via wallpaperCyclingFolderPath
in the DMS default session (prev/next flips through the set; cycling is off by
default). shrek-swamp-default.jpg is the shipped default, kept here so users can
return to it.
